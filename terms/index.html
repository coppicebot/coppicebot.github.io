Coppice Terms of Service

Last updated: November 25, 2026

These terms cover your use of Coppice, a Discord bot that watches Minecraft servers. Coppice ("we") is run from Georgia, USA. By adding Coppice to a Discord server or using its commands, you agree to them. Questions and legal notices: support@coppicebot.com.

1. What Coppice does

Coppice checks the Minecraft servers you add from the outside, again and again, posts a status card, sends alerts when a server stops answering, and draws graphs from what it has seen. What it can see is what any player's multiplayer screen can see.

Coppice is built and run with AI assistance. A person owns it and is accountable for it. The Privacy Policy says how AI is used.

2. It can be wrong or late

Coppice can miss an outage, alert late, or alert when the server is fine, for example if the network between us and your server has trouble, or if Discord or our host is down. Don't rely on Coppice as your only way to know about something important. Coppice is provided "as is", without warranties of any kind, to the extent the law allows.

3. Fair use

  • Only add servers you run or are allowed to watch.
  • Don't use Coppice to harass anyone, to overload a server, or to probe addresses that aren't Minecraft servers.
  • Don't try to get around plan limits, abuse referrals, or interfere with the bot or its server.

We may stop watching a server or remove Coppice from a Discord that breaks these rules.

4. Free and Premium

Coppice is free for up to 10 servers per Discord server, with 24-hour, 7-day and 30-day graphs. Premium costs $4.99 a month per Discord server and adds up to 25 servers, 90-day graphs and history, up to 5 patch-note channels with snapshots, and your own alert wording, as listed in /coppice premium. Checks and alerts are the same on both plans. Premium is not on sale yet.

  • Premium is bought through Discord's own store. Discord takes the payment, and Discord's terms and refund policy apply to it.
  • You can cancel in Discord any time. Premium lasts until the end of the period you paid for.
  • If Premium ends, servers over the free limit pause (the newest first) and start again when Premium is back. Graph history older than 31 days is deleted within the hour. Nothing else is deleted.
  • We may change prices or features. Changes to Premium apply from your next renewal and are announced at least 30 days ahead.

5. Referrals

Each Discord's referral code is shown by /coppice premium. A Discord that added Coppice in the last 14 days can enter another Discord's code with /coppice redeem. If Coppice is still in that Discord and watching at least one server 14 days later, both Discords get 30 days of Premium. A Discord can be referred only once, and a referring Discord can earn at most 6 rewards. Premium days from referrals have no cash value and can't be sold or moved. We may void referrals that look like abuse.

6. Your content

Server addresses, nicknames and alert wording you give Coppice stay yours. You let us store and show them in your Discord to run the bot. Don't put anything in them that you don't have the right to share. The Privacy Policy (https://coppicebot.com/privacy/) says what we store and for how long.

7. Changes and ending

We may change or stop Coppice. If we shut it down, we'll say so on coppicebot.com at least 30 days ahead where we can, and no new Premium charges will be made. You can stop using Coppice any time by removing it from your Discord.
